Modify the existing Xtream UTD UI/UX design into a premium, immersive product-catalogue website for a gadget and accessories business.

The website represents Xtream UTD, a business selling trendy gadgets, desk accessories, lifestyle accessories, tech products, decorative products, and useful everyday items.

This is not a creative-agency portfolio website.

Replace all references to:

* Projects
* Portfolio
* Case studies
* Clients
* Creative work

With relevant product-commerce terminology such as:

* Products
* Product catalogue
* Product details
* Categories
* Featured products
* New arrivals
* Best sellers
* Product specifications

The website should allow visitors to explore products visually, view all products in a conventional catalogue, open individual product pages, see prices, and contact Xtream UTD to place an order.

Do not create production code yet.

Create a complete, editable, developer-ready UI/UX design.

# 1. PRIMARY WEBSITE GOAL

The website should help customers:

* Discover Xtream UTD
* Explore products through an immersive 3D product view
* Browse all available products
* Filter products by category
* View product prices
* Open individual product detail pages
* Check product features and specifications
* Contact the business
* Place an order through Messenger, WhatsApp, phone, or another configurable order method

The website should feel:

* Premium
* Modern
* Trendy
* Trustworthy
* Product-focused
* Minimal
* Visually engaging
* Easy to navigate
* Mobile-friendly
* Suitable for a gadget and accessories store

# 2. HEADER DESIGN

Create a fixed or floating header.

Place the company name or wordmark at the far-left corner:

Xtream UTD

Immediately beside the company name, show the main navigation options:

* Home
* All Products
* Categories
* About
* Contact

On the right side, include:

* Search button
* Theme switcher
* Mobile menu button
* Optional shopping bag or order-list icon

The Xtream UTD wordmark should always return the user to the homepage.

The Home button should be visibly active when the visitor is on the homepage.

Use a compact, premium header that does not cover important products in the interactive view.

# 3. HOMEPAGE — INTERACTIVE PRODUCT VIEW

The homepage should contain an interactive three-dimensional product world inspired by the reference video.

Instead of portfolio projects, display actual product cards distributed around a virtual sphere or spatial environment.

Each floating product card should contain:

* Product image
* Short product title
* Price
* Optional discount badge
* Availability status

Products should appear at different sizes and depths.

Users should be able to:

* Drag to rotate the product world
* Scroll to zoom in and out
* Pinch to zoom on mobile
* Hover over products
* Tap products on mobile
* Select a product
* Open its product-detail page

Keep the controls, header, and bottom navigation fixed while the product world moves.

# 4. HOMEPAGE TOP CONTENT

At the upper-left portion of the homepage, include:

Xtream UTD

“Trending gadgets and accessories for your desk, home and everyday life.”

* Quality products
* Affordable prices
* Delivery across Bangladesh

All wording must remain editable.

Do not let the introduction occupy too much space.

# 5. HOMEPAGE BOTTOM NAVIGATION

Create a floating pill-shaped navigation control at the bottom centre of the screen.

Include two primary options:

* Explore Products
* View All Products

Explore Products should keep the visitor inside the interactive 3D product view.

View All Products should redirect the visitor to the complete product catalogue page.

The active option should be clearly visible through typography, background, indicator, or underline.

Recommended behaviour:

* Explore Products → `/`
* View All Products → `/products`

On mobile, keep the control large enough for comfortable tapping.

* Show a “View Details” action
* Change the cursor to indicate clickability

# 7. PRODUCT CLICK BEHAVIOUR

When a visitor clicks a product card:

1. Distinguish the click from a drag action.
2. Highlight the selected product.
3. Dim surrounding products.
4. Show a short transition.
5. Redirect the visitor to that product’s detail page.

Use a route structure such as:

`/products/portable-folding-laptop-stand`

When the visitor returns to the homepage, restore their previous rotation, zoom level, and browsing position where technically possible.
