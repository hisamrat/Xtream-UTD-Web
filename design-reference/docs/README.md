# Xtream UTD Product UI/UX Design Pack

This pack converts Xtream UTD from a creative portfolio concept into a product-catalogue experience for gadgets and accessories.

## Contents
- `editable_svg/` - editable SVG boards importable into Figma, Penpot, Sketch or Illustrator
- `preview_png/` - rendered previews
- `data/sample_products.json` - 24 realistic editable product records
- `docs/Xtream_UTD_Product_UIUX_Specification.docx` - developer-ready design specification
- `docs/Xtream_UTD_Product_UIUX_Preview.pdf` - PDF preview generated from the DOCX
- `docs/interaction_acceptance_criteria.md` - implementation acceptance criteria

## Editing notes
- All SVG text, colour, vector product illustrations and layout elements remain editable.
- Product art is original placeholder vector artwork and should be replaced with Xtream UTD product photography.
- Real 3D rotation, inertia, zoom and WebGL behaviour are specified but are implemented during development.
- Messenger, WhatsApp, phone and policy details remain editable placeholders until verified.
